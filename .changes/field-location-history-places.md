titulo: Nearby place names in field location history
---

Field Sales → Team → Location history now shows a compact, paged list of recorded
GPS fixes. Each row includes the Dubai time, a nearby area or city from the
installed map archive, coordinates, GPS accuracy, and a map link. Flagged or
mock locations remain marked. If the archive has no nearby label, the row says
so while retaining the recorded coordinates.
