Adds data, automation, CRM, booking-link, payment-link and staff-notification adapters to the organization-isolated integrations framework. Builder actions can save projected scalar results into session variables. Signed inbound and Stripe checkout events are recorded with duplicate protection and available by event ID. Mailchimp subscription reads, Dialogflow intent detection and Segment analytics are supported. External synthetic-test effects are explicitly disclosed. Messenger and final release verification remain pending; no customer bots are automatically published.
