titulo: Retail lead entry with one open Event ID per phone
---
Salespeople can capture retail customer details and a required next follow-up in a dedicated form. Managers assign each salesperson's store; the CRM fills it from the logged-in user. An organization cannot create another open Event ID for the same phone until the earlier lead is Converted or Lost.

Completing a follow-up records the next status and due date. Managers see overdue and escalated leads after one Monday–Friday working day in the organization's timezone. A manager who belongs to both organizations can move an open Event ID to another brand, retaining its ID, customer and notes. Leads with other linked records require review before transfer; the move fails without changing either organization.
